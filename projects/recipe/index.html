Buttermilk Pancakes

These buttermilk pancakes are soft, fluffy, and easy to make for breakfast. Learn more about pancakes on Wikipedia.

Introduction

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup melted butter
  • Butter or cooking oil for the pan
  • Maple syrup for serving

Cooking Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir gently until combined. A few small lumps are okay.
  4. Heat a skillet or griddle over medium heat and lightly coat it with butter or cooking oil.
  5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter onto the skillet for each pancake.
  6. Cook for about 2 to 3 minutes, or until bubbles form on the surface and the edges begin to look dry.
  7. Flip each pancake and cook for another 1 to 2 minutes, until the second side is golden brown.
  8. Remove the pancakes from the skillet and repeat until all of the batter is used.
  9. Serve the pancakes warm with butter and maple syrup.

About The Recipe

This is a simple homemade pancake recipe that works well for breakfast or brunch. Do not overmix the batter, because this can make the pancakes less fluffy. Recipe based on a homemade family-style pancake recipe.
